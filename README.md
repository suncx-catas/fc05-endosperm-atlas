# One Endosperm, Divergent Metabolic Trajectories — Multi-Tissue Developmental Metabolome Atlas of Coconut Fruit

**Affiliation:** Coconut Research Institute, Chinese Academy of Tropical Agricultural Sciences (CRI-CATAS), Wenchang, Hainan, China  
**Target Journal:** *Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry* (JAFC)  
**Version:** FC-05 V11.1 (2026-06-18)

## Highlights

- **Dual-Platform Multi-Tissue Atlas:** Profiles 12,947 metabolic features across three varieties (W5, W6, CK) and nine developmental time points—combining targeted UPLC-MS/MS for coconut water (624 metabolites, 6 stages) and untargeted UHPLC-Q-TOF-MS for kernel (12,323 features, 3 stages).
- **Opposing Tissue Trajectories:** 8 of 12 overlapping metabolite classes move in opposite directions between liquid coconut water and solid kernel during the cellularization switch (~7 months).
- **Reversed Polarity of Flavonoids & Tannins:** Flavonoids and tannins are depleted in liquid endosperm (log₂ FC = −1.42 and −3.08) yet actively accumulated in solid kernel (+0.16 and +0.55 in W6), whereas amino acids, nucleotides, and organic acids follow the inverse trajectory.
- **Actionable Harvest Optimization Calendar:** Temporal profiling of 173 flavor- and nutrition-relevant compounds defines quantitative harvest windows—6–8 months for beverage-grade water (7.5–8.2 Brix°), 10–12 months for functional water (peak free amino acids), and 12 months for virgin coconut oil (peak lipids + phenolics).

## Abstract

Coconut water and kernel derive from a single endosperm yet differ radically in composition: water is a sugary beverage, while kernel is an oil-rich solid. Using targeted UPLC-MS/MS (coconut water, 624 metabolites, 6 time points, 2 varieties) and untargeted UHPLC-Q-TOF-MS (kernel, 12,323 features, 3 time points, 3 varieties), we show that 8 of 12 overlapping metabolite classes move in opposite directions between water and kernel during development. Flavonoids and tannins are the only classes with reversed polarity (depleted in water, accumulated in kernel), while amino acids, nucleotides, and organic acids accumulate in water and decline in kernel. Temporal profiling of 173 flavor- and nutrition-relevant compounds establishes variety-specific harvest optimization guidelines for beverage, functional food, and virgin coconut oil markets.
